THE BEGINNING

More than a reading.

The Zentarot Co. is an independent spiritual project built around cards, symbols, books and personal interpretation. Since 2020, every reading begins with your question.

HANDMADE

Made with time, energy and intention.

Every request receives real time, concentration, commitment, energy and personal attention.

Nothing is simply copied or automatically generated. Physical tarot and oracle cards are used. Books are opened. Symbols are observed. The question is studied and kept in mind while the reading is developed.

Each tradition has its own history, language and symbolism. The interpretation is built around the individual question and its context.

The final result is written and prepared specifically for the person who asked.

THE PRACTICES

Explore the symbolic languages.

Every system has its own history, symbols and method. Your question remains at the centre of the experience.

01 / TAROT

Tarot

Ask what you want to explore. There are no rigid question categories. Write your question and, if useful, the context behind it.

Physical cards are drawn and interpreted through symbolism, position and their relationship with the question.

02 / I CHING

I Ching

The Book of Changes is approached through its hexagrams, lines and symbolic structure.

The question is considered first, then the book and the resulting hexagram become part of the interpretation.

03 / ORACLE

Oracle

Physical oracle cards bring together imagery, themes, symbols and messages.

Each card is considered in relation to the question and the overall reading.

04 / SIBILLE

Sibille

A traditional cartomantic language built around scenes, characters and recognizable symbols.

The cards are connected together to develop an interpretation around the situation presented.

05 / RITUALS

Rituals

Symbolic spiritual practices centred around intention, focus and personal meaning.

They may explore themes such as love, luck and personal intention, without practices intended to harm another person.

06 / YOUR QUESTION

Your Question

You do not need to find the perfect category. Explain what is on your mind in your own words.

The reading begins from there.

I CHING

The Book of Changes.

The I Ching is traditionally known as the Book of Changes, a symbolic system built around 64 hexagrams and changing lines.

The physical book is part of the experience: the question is considered, the hexagram is consulted, and its symbolism is explored in relation to the situation presented.

RITUAL WORK

Intention becomes ritual.

Ritual work is presented as a symbolic spiritual practice: a focused moment dedicated to intention, reflection and personal meaning.
