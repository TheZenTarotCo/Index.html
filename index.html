The story behind the cards

Every system has a story.

Understanding where a tradition comes from helps us understand the symbols we work with today.

Tarot

Tarot cards emerged in Renaissance Italy as playing cards before later becoming associated with occult and divinatory traditions. Modern Tarot therefore carries both a historical card-game heritage and centuries of later symbolic interpretation.

I Ching

The I Ching is one of the oldest Chinese classical texts and is traditionally associated with 64 hexagrams representing patterns of change and transformation.

Oracle & Sibille

Oracle decks developed in many different forms, while Sibille-style cards became popular through European fortune-telling traditions. Their images create different symbolic languages for readings.

Ancient wisdom

The Book of Changes.

The I Ching offers a symbolic language built around change, movement and transformation.
