The Zentarot Co. is a digital spiritual reading space created around one simple idea: sometimes a different perspective can change the way we see a question.

Through Tarot, I Ching, Oracle and Sibille traditions, each reading is created as a personal moment of reflection, symbolism and discovery.

The practices

Different systems.
One journey.

Explore different symbolic traditions, each offering its own language and perspective.

01 · TAROT

Tarot

A symbolic system of archetypes, images and patterns. Tarot readings can be used to explore questions, relationships, situations and personal perspectives.

02 · I CHING

I Ching

An ancient Chinese divination tradition centered around change, balance and the movement of circumstances. A different way to approach a question and its possibilities.

03 · ORACLE

Oracle

Oracle cards offer a more open symbolic language, allowing themes, messages and intuitive interpretations to emerge around a specific situation.

04 · SIBILLE

Sibille

A traditional card system with a distinctive visual vocabulary, often used to explore everyday situations, interactions and possible developments.
