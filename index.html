THE BEGINNING

More than a reading.

The Zentarot Co. is an independent spiritual project created around the ancient language of cards, symbols and books. Since 2020, every reading begins with one thing: your question.

HANDMADE

Nothing is simply generated.

Every request receives time, concentration, intention and personal energy.

The cards are physically consulted. Books are opened. Symbols are observed. The question is studied and kept in mind while the reading is developed.

Tarot, Oracle, Sibille and I Ching are approached through their own symbolic traditions, with the interpretation built around the individual question and context.

The final reading is then written and prepared specifically for the person who asked.

✦
THE PRACTICES

Four traditions.
One personal experience.

Each system has its own symbols, history and way of approaching a question. The reading is shaped around what you choose to ask.

01 / TAROT

Tarot

Ask what you want to explore. There are no rigid categories: simply write your question and, when useful, the context behind it.

The cards are drawn and interpreted through their traditional symbolism, positions and relationship with the question.

02 / I CHING

I Ching

The Book of Changes is approached through its hexagrams, lines and symbolic structure.

The question is considered first, then the physical book and the resulting hexagram become part of the interpretation.

03 / ORACLE

Oracle

Oracle cards use imagery, themes and symbolic messages to create a different style of reading.

Each card is considered in relation to the question, surrounding cards and the overall message.

04 / SIBILLE

Sibille

A traditional cartomantic language built around recognizable scenes, characters and everyday symbols.

Individual cards are connected together to develop a reading around the situation presented.

05 / RITUALS

Rituals

Symbolic spiritual practices created around intention, focus and personal meaning.

The work may be connected with themes such as love, luck and personal intention, without practices aimed at harming another person.

06 / PERSONAL

Your Question

You do not need to choose a complicated category. Write what is on your mind and explain the situation in your own words.

The reading begins from there.

THE BOOK OF CHANGES

Ancient symbols.
Personal questions.

The I Ching is traditionally known as the Book of Changes. Its system is built around 64 hexagrams and changing lines, each carrying its own symbolic structure.

Here, the physical book is part of the experience: the question is considered first, the hexagram is consulted, and the symbolism is then explored in relation to your situation.

RITUAL WORK

Intention becomes ritual.

Ritual work is presented as a symbolic spiritual practice: a focused moment dedicated to intention, reflection and the meaning you give to your goal.
