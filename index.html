Ancient wisdom

The Book of Changes.

The I Ching has been used for centuries as a symbolic system of reflection and change. Its hexagrams create a visual language through which a personal situation can be explored.

A question becomes a pattern. A pattern becomes something to contemplate.

Symbolic practice

Intention becomes ritual.

Our rituals are created around symbolic themes such as love, luck, intention and personal focus. They are meant as spiritual and symbolic practices, not as guaranteed ways to control people or events.

We do not offer rituals intended to cause harm, curses, physical harm or health-related outcomes.
